Live at RISKPROP Realty

Alec answers RISKPROP's tenants on WhatsApp, day and night.

He is the number on the agency's Property24 adverts. He reads the live stock list, quotes real prices, sends unit photos and books viewings, then tells the agent on duty. When he gets something wrong, I watch it happen in the chat log and fix it.

  1. Monday 12 October, 15:15

    A message arrives with nothing but a listing number.

    It came from a Property24 advert, but Alec had no way to match the number to a unit. He asked a sensible question, and the person had to explain where they came from.

  2. The fix

    Every unit now carries its advert number.

    The team pastes it into the Stock Manager once, next to the rent and the photos. Alec reads it from the same place, so he knows which advert a person clicked before they say a word.

  3. Tuesday 13 October, 18:17

    The next enquiry gets a straight answer.

    Alec opens with the building, the unit and the price of the advert the person tapped. No back and forth to work out what they meant.

The systems behind it

Alec is one part. The rest runs the agency day to day.

Everything below reads from the same database, so the team never has two numbers that disagree.

  • Stock Manager Live

    Where the team prices, edits and switches units on and off. Bulk rent increases with a preview first, a record of who changed what, and three levels of access.

  • Public website Live

    Listings come straight from the Stock Manager and disappear the moment a unit is let. It replaced the agency's paid website vendor.

  • ROY, utilities checks In use

    Takes the monthly municipal and electricity figures the team captures and lists the bills that look wrong, so management can check them and mark them fixed.

  • Team board In use

    One shared board for six departments, from cleaning to accounts, so the owner and the team look at the same picture.

Built with n8n, Supabase, React, the WhatsApp Business API and OpenAI models. I pick the tools to suit the job.

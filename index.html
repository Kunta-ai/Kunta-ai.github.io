Everything below reads from the same database, so the team never has two numbers that disagree.

  • Stock Manager Live

    Where the team prices, edits and switches units on and off. Bulk rent increases with a preview first, a record of who changed what, and three levels of access.

  • Public website Live

    Listings come straight from the Stock Manager and disappear the moment a unit is let. It replaced the agency's paid website vendor.

  • ROY, utilities checks In use

    Takes the monthly municipal and electricity figures the team captures and lists the bills that look wrong, so management can check them and mark them fixed.

  • Team board In use

    One shared board for six departments, from cleaning to accounts, so the owner and the team look at the same picture.
